InstaNovo-FMembedding explorer

A self-supervised encoder for tandem mass spectra, trained by hiding peaks rather than predicting peptide sequences. Below is its embedding space: 100,000 held-out spectra, placed only by what the model learned from the spectra themselves. Colour by instrument, fragmentation, labelling chemistry or retention time — none of which the model was told. Manuscript in preparation.
